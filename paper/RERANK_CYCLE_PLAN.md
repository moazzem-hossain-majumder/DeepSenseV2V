# Stages 96–116: constrained shortlist reranking

Stage 96 audits training and validation coverage of geometry's first 5, 10 and 20 beams, including scenario breakdowns and missed-near-optimal counts. A shortlist size qualifies for pilots only if its training macro coverage exceeds the top-five training coverage by at least two percentage points. This coverage is an optimistic ceiling for choosing five candidates; the oracle is never an input to inference. The baseline protocol audit is recorded separately.

Stages 97–100 compare K=10 and K=20 with matched GPS-only and GPS-plus-both-camera rerankers. Each pilot uses seed 42 and three full epochs. The frozen pretrained ResNet-18 produces normalized 512-dimensional clip features; no visual encoder updates occur. The GPS control uses zeros in the image feature slots, with the same trainable scoring head. Camera clips retain the previous side-by-side front/rear layout. Historical GPS/context and candidate beam descriptors feed a shared small head. Initial residuals are zero. FP32 softsign residuals have magnitude below 2, and a hard mask prevents any beam outside the GPS shortlist entering the first K ranks. Power supervision is restricted to conditional candidate ranking during training; it is absent from inference. The objective uses conditional soft-label cross-entropy, expected candidate-relative regret and a residual penalty. It is a surrogate, not direct optimization of the top-five success metric.

Stage 101 expands only a visual pilot that exceeds both geometry and its matched GPS pilot by at least 0.25 percentage points on selection validation. If neither qualifies, stages 102–115 are marked skipped and the report is still produced.

Stages 102–107 train both matched models afresh for eight epochs at the selected K with seeds 42, 7 and 13. Stages 108–115 train both models for each held scenario, excluding that scenario from fitting, scaling and checkpoint-selection validation. Stage 116 reports all pilots, paired macro trajectory-bootstrap intervals against geometry and the GPS control, and transfer results. K/model choice used pooled validation, so subsequent transfer checks are exploratory, not independent confirmation.

Matched budgets: batch 16, existing AdamW and schedule, unchanged partitions and preprocessing. Backbone frozen/eval mode, head trained. No new download, timestamp shifting, calibration or test evaluation. Metrics above K include geometry-ordered masked-out beams and are secondary diagnostics; only shortlist-contained k values should support reranking claims.

The existing native progress window and 15-second supervisor run stages automatically. Checkpoints resume completed epochs, repeated failures stop for attention, and sign-in recovery restores the queue. `pause.flag` pauses at epoch/stage boundaries. Preliminary estimate: 30–90 minutes through pilots, 2–5 hours total if matched expansion proceeds. Outputs: `results/rerank_v1/`; final report: `stage116/REPORT.md`. No publication readiness is inferred automatically.
