## Claim supported by the experiments

For this full-codebook adaptation, on the four explored DeepSense-V2V scenarios, adding RGB changed the share of all excluded-scenario windows that succeeded through an alternative beam by −25.28 percentage points (paired block interval −30.64 to −15.86). The exact-hit point estimate increased by 5.42 points with an interval spanning zero. The separately measured 84.7% candidate-exclusion share pertains to severe misses of the 20-candidate geometry-constrained architecture. The original pooled-minus-transfer contrast for the full-codebook model was inconclusive. The alternative-beam decomposition was post hoc. Four explored scenarios and few recording blocks constrain inference.

## Closest prior art

- Fabiani et al. already forecast V2V beams from GPS and front/rear cameras using shifted-window fusion, and report both exact-beam accuracy and power loss. Architecture, sensor combination and use of both endpoints are established. [Published paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/IEEEConf60004.2024.10942842)
- Mollah et al. already compared fused and unimodal models on the same four scenarios, using a 64-beam task and random 6:2:2 partitions within each scenario. The present study's paired leave-one-scenario-out design and decomposition of near-optimal alternatives provide a narrower point of distinction. [Published paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/GLOBECOM59602.2025.11432270) · [Experimental setup, section IV](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.11112v1)
- BeamBench evaluated multimodal predictions under distribution shift. Wang et al. examined cross-environment label-space shift and proposed few-shot adaptation using measured power for pairwise ranking. These studies rule out a broad novelty claim about cross-environment evaluation or power-informed ranking. [BeamBench author manuscript](https://alloulah.github.io/docs/Arnold22_BeamBench.pdf) · [Wang et al., PIMRC 2026](https://doi.org/10.1109/PIMRC69400.2026.11711825)
- The challenge already scored power loss. Its citation guidance requests the dataset papers and the corresponding position and vision studies. The TVT copy includes them. [Challenge and citation guidance](https://deepsense6g.net/beam_prediction_challenge_2023)
