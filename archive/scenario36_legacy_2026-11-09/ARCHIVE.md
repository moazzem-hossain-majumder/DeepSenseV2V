# Legacy Scenario 36 archive

Archived on 2026-11-09 at the user's request. This preserves the earlier Scenario 36-only work while removing its artifacts from the active multi-scenario project locations.

**56 original files were moved and their SHA-256 hashes verified after the move.** [MANIFEST.json](MANIFEST.json) lists every original relative path, byte size and hash. The directory layout preserves those relative paths.

Contents include the former README, combined EDA/results notebook, eight notebook stages, Word report and draft, paper diagrams, EDA plots, historical model-result JSONs and comparison tables, single-scenario split CSVs, and three standalone legacy scripts.

Raw data, current four-scenario EDA, current checkpoints, active queue files and shared source dependencies were not moved. The older V4 design document includes multi-scenario planning and remains outside this archive.

These artifacts describe historical experiments and do not establish current multi-scenario results. Archived scripts/notebooks are preserved as references; their original working-directory and relative-path assumptions have not been rewritten. Use the manifest to restore selected files to their original locations if needed, taking care not to overwrite newer work.
